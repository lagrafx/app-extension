# Backlog working agreement

GitHub Issues are the source of truth for this repository's backlog. Use the **Backlog item** issue template. This guide deliberately contains no duplicate list of work items.

## Required properties
Each issue records its title and permanent repository-qualified GitHub issue number/URL; work type (bug, enhancement, technical debt, or investigation); provisional SAFe planning level (Feature or Story); real parent Feature when applicable; user need and business benefit; scope and exclusions; acceptance criteria and required tests; evidence/reproduction/confidence; suggested priority, dependencies, risks, and owner; approval status, approver, and date; delivery status, branch, and PR; ServiceNow ID/link; and either a Feature benefit hypothesis or Story wording with team-estimated points.

The issue number and canonical URL generated by GitHub are the permanent identifier. Keep links rather than copying the same backlog into this document. A Story does not require an invented parent Feature. Only the delivery team estimates story points.

## Proposal and approval
New items start with **Approval: Needs review** and **Delivery: Proposed**. Owner, estimate, approver, and approval date remain unset. Suggested priority and planning level are explicitly provisional, not approved commitments.

Issue creation authorizes recording a proposal only, never implementation. Human approval is role-separated from implementation: the designated human approver explicitly reviews scope, acceptance criteria, risk, and priority. Record their decision, identity, date, and a link to the approval evidence. Do not automatically assign the owner, repository owner, author, or implementer as approver. A changed scope or material risk needs renewed review.

Approval statuses: Needs review, Approved, Rejected, or Needs revision.
Delivery statuses: Proposed, Ready, In progress, Blocked, In review, or Done.
Labels may assist navigation; they do not enforce approval or grant authority.

## Ready and backlog-menu eligibility
Only offer or claim work that is **Approved + Ready + unclaimed + has no overlapping PR + has all dependencies met**. Immediately before claiming, live-recheck the issue body and approval evidence, assignees/owner, dependencies, and open PRs/linked branches. Resolve conflicting ownership or overlap before starting; record owner/claim and linked branch/PR only under authorization.

Ready means scope and acceptance tests are clear, dependencies are resolved, risks reviewed, and the required human approval is recorded. Never infer approval from issue existence, labels, a suggested priority, or a proposed estimate.

## Delivery and closure
Implementation requires its own authorization. Link the branch and PR to the issue; preserve the permanent issue identity. Verify required tests and acceptance criteria, record results and any limitations, and have completion reviewed before marking Done. This backlog setup does not authorize application changes, merges, deployments, or automation.

## ServiceNow interoperability
Keep ServiceNow ID and link unset until a verified record exists. Exact field mapping and synchronization remain pending the user's ServiceNow screenshots; do not invent field names, approvals, relationships, or create external records.
